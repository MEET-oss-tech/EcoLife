DAILY TIPS

Small habits, better impact

Try one simple idea at a time and slowly build better habits.

TIP 01

Carry a reusable bottle instead of buying single-use plastic bottles.

01

Save Energy

Switch off lights, fans and devices when they are not needed.

02

Reduce Plastic

Carry reusable bags and containers whenever possible.

03

Reuse More

Before throwing something away, think about whether it can be reused.

04

Buy Carefully

Choose useful products instead of buying things you may not need.

05

Travel Smart

Walk, cycle or use public transport for suitable short trips.

06

Reduce Food Waste

Plan meals and use leftovers before they go to waste.
